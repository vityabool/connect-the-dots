# Code of Conduct

This project follows the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/)
(licensed under [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/)). This is a short adaptation.

## Our pledge

We want participation in this project, in issues, pull requests and the hosted game, to be a harassment-free experience for
everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of
experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual
identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Be kind, patient and respectful. Assume good intent.
- Be specific and constructive when you complain or disagree. Criticize the work, not the people.
- Accept that this is a free project maintained in spare time: there is no obligation to answer, fix or accept anything.
- Respect privacy: never share other people's personal data.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, personal attacks, discriminatory jokes or language, sexualized content, trolling.
- Publishing others' private information (for example real names from a game, email addresses) without permission.
- Demanding support, deadlines or work from maintainers or volunteers, or repeated pressure after a request was declined.
- Using the hosted game to harass or embarrass people (see the terms of service).

## Enforcement

The maintainers may edit or remove comments, issues, pull requests and other contributions that do not follow this Code of
Conduct, may lock conversations, and may temporarily or permanently ban people from the project. Games that are used to
harass people may be closed or deleted.

## Reporting

Report conduct problems to the maintainers privately by email, at the contact address published in the
[privacy policy](https://www.connect-the-dots.top/privacy#contact). All reports are treated with
discretion.
